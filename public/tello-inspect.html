Suggestions for Accurate AI Results

Because AI photo analysis relies heavily on lighting, framing, and clear context, a few simple habits improve report accuracy.

Match Your Move-In AnglesTake photos from the exact same corners and distances during move-in and move-out so Tello's comparison engine can spot subtle changes easily.
Narrate Functional ChecksPhotos cannot show water pressure, slow drains, or wobbly handles on their own. State aloud when a faucet drips, a toilet rocks, or an appliance hums so Tello includes it in the written report.
Use Bright, Even LightingTurn on all overhead lights and open blinds before snapping photos to prevent shadows from looking like water stains or wall scuffs.
Pair Wide Shots with Close-UpsStart each room with a wide corner shot for spatial context, then take tight close-ups of high-wear fixtures like faucets, locks, and appliances.
