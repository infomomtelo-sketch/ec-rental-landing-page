What to put on a small business website FAQ page

A good FAQ page saves you time and helps customers decide. Here is what to include and how to write it.

Start with the questions you already hear

Write down the last 20 questions customers asked by phone, email, text or in person. Those are your FAQ. Do not invent questions nobody asks.

Questions most small businesses should answer

  • What do you do, and who is it for?
  • Where do you work, or what area do you serve?
  • What are your hours?
  • How much does it cost, or how do you price?
  • How do I book or get a quote?
  • How can I pay?
  • What is your cancellation or change policy?
  • How do I contact you?

How to write the answers

  1. Lead with the answerPut the answer in the first sentence, then add detail.
  2. Keep it shortOne to three sentences is enough for most answers.
  3. Use plain wordsWrite the way you would say it to a customer.
  4. Link to the next stepPoint to your booking page, contact form or price list.

Keep it current

Review your FAQ every few months and whenever your prices, hours or policies change. An out-of-date answer is worse than no answer.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions should an FAQ page have?

Include the questions customers really ask. For most small businesses that is between 6 and 15.

Where should I put my FAQ page?

Link it from your main menu or footer, and link to it from your contact and pricing pages.

Does an FAQ page help with search?

Clear, honest answers help people and can help search engines understand your page. Write for customers first.
