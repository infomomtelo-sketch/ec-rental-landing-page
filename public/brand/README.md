# Tello brand kit

Mark: a gold speech bubble with a T cut out, and a small mint "live" dot, on a deep green tile.
The bubble is the assistant that answers, the T is Tello, and the dot says it is live right now.

## Colors
- Deep green `#123a30` (tile and backgrounds)
- Ink `#0b1f19` (dark backgrounds)
- Gold `#e8a23d` (bubble, ".live" and buttons)
- Cream `#f9f5ed` (text on dark)
- Mint `#46d39a` (the live dot only)

## Files
- `tello-mark.svg`: the mark on its green tile (use for apps, favicons, avatars)
- `tello-mark-bare.svg`: the mark without the tile, for dark backgrounds
- `tello-mark-mono-dark.svg` / `tello-mark-mono-light.svg`: one-color versions for stamps, print and embroidery
- `tello-logo-horizontal-on-dark.svg` / `-on-light.svg`: mark plus "tello.live"
- `tello-logo-stacked-on-dark.svg` / `-on-light.svg`: mark above "tello.live"
- `tello-avatar-1024.png`: profile picture for every social network (square, platforms crop it to a circle)
- `tello-icon-1024/512/192/48/32.png`, `tello-apple-touch-180.png`: app and website icons
- `tello-x-header-1500x500.png`, `tello-linkedin-banner-1584x396.png`, `tello-facebook-cover-1640x624.png`, `tello-youtube-banner-2560x1440.png`: cover images
- `tello-og-1200x630.png`: link share preview
- `tello-post-square-1080.png`: square post for Instagram and Facebook

## Rules
- The name is "Tello". The site is written tello.live, with ".live" in gold.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of the T's stem.
- Do not stretch, recolor or add effects. Do not use the mint dot on its own.
- Never put the mark on a busy photo without a green tile behind it.
- The wordmark in the SVG files is converted to outlines from Inter ExtraBold, so no font is needed.
