Meet Tello, the AI inside EC Rental

Your rental assistant that never sleeps

Tello works around the clock so renters get answers and landlords get time back. A real person always makes the final call.

  • Answers renters 24/7 about rent, pets, move-in dates and showings, right on each listing.
  • Writes your listing from a few facts, and checks your rent against the area.
  • Drafts replies to renter inquiries and summarizes applications for you to review.
  • Reads inspection photos room by room, flags repairs and compares move-out with move-in.
  • Sorts maintenance requests by urgency so the important ones come first.

About Us

Real people behind the software

EC Rental Property Management LLC builds AI-powered tools for landlords and property managers, backed by real, hands-on property management experience. Whether you own one rental or manage a growing portfolio, the platform and Tello work for you wherever your properties are.

We are based in Fresno, California, where we also manage rentals directly and run in-person move-in, move-out and routine inspections across Fresno and Clovis.

Our mission is simple: protect your investment, keep quality tenants happy, and deliver peace of mind to property owners. Whether you own a single rental home or a multi-unit portfolio, we provide personalized, professional service every step of the way.

Our property inspections are led by a certified home inspector, trained through Home Inspectors of America, and backed by AI that documents every room with photos.
