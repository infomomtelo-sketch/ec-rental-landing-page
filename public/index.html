About Us

EC Rental Property Management LLC is a locally owned and operated property management company serving Fresno, California and the surrounding Central Valley. We understand the local rental market and California landlord-tenant laws inside and out.

Our mission is simple: protect your investment, keep quality tenants happy, and deliver peace of mind to property owners. Whether you own a single rental home or a multi-unit portfolio, we provide personalized, professional service every step of the way.
