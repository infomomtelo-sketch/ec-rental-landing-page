Inspections we do

Move-in

A dated, room-by-room record of the home's condition before the tenant moves in, signed by both sides.

Move-out

The same rooms photographed again and compared with move-in. Each change is marked as likely wear and tear, possible damage or unclear, for the landlord to review.

Routine and annual

Catch leaks, worn parts and safety issues early. Any repair found can become a maintenance request in one tap.

How it works

  1. We photograph every room and area of the home.
  2. AI drafts a condition note for each photo and flags anything that needs repair.
  3. The inspector reviews and corrects every note.
  4. You get a printable report with photos, a repair list and signature lines, saved in your dashboard.

Why it matters for deposits in California

California Civil Code 1950.5 lets a landlord deduct from a security deposit for damage beyond ordinary wear and tear, and requires an itemized statement. A dated move-in report with photos, compared against move-out, makes those decisions clearer and fairer for both landlord and tenant.
