How to make a short business video

You do not need a studio. You need one message, a short script and a clear next step.

Keep it short and focused

Aim for 30 to 60 seconds with one message. If you have three messages, make three videos.

A simple structure

  1. 1. The problemOpen with the problem your customer has, in their words.
  2. 2. Your answerShow what you do and how it helps, in two or three short points.
  3. 3. The next stepFinish with one action: visit, call, book or sign up.

Make it easy to watch

  • Add captions, since many people watch without sound
  • Use a clear voice and plain words
  • Pick a shape that fits where you will post: vertical for phones and stories, wide for websites
  • Show your web address at the end

Frequently asked questions

How long should a business video be?

For most small businesses, 30 to 60 seconds with a single message works well.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. Narrated videos with captions and simple scenes work well for many businesses.

What should I say at the end of the video?

Give one clear next step, such as your web address, a phone number or a booking link.
