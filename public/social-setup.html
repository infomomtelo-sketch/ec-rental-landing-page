Questions

Does Tello create the accounts for me?

No. Each network requires the account owner to sign up, and automated sign-ups get banned. Tello gives you the names, bios, pictures and the steps, and you do the sign-up in a few minutes.

Is anything saved?

No. The helper runs in your browser. What you type is not sent to Tello or stored.

Which networks are covered?

Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, YouTube and TikTok.
