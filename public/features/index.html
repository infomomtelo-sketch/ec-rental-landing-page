The EC Rental platform

Everything your rentals need. Tello built in.

Eight tools that work together, from the first listing to tax season, for Fresno and Clovis landlords and the renters they serve.

Tello AI assistant

Tello works inside every part of EC Rental. It answers renters at midnight, writes your listing from a few facts, reads inspection photos and sorts repairs by urgency. You stay in charge of every decision.

Listings + Zillow feed

Every home gets its own fast listing page with photos, a map and an Apply button. Tello writes the description, checks your rent and answers renters around the clock.

Online applications

Renters apply from any listing on their phone. You see every application in one place, with a quick AI summary to help you review, and the decision always stays with you.

AI inspections

Snap photos room by room. Tello fills in the condition and notes, a certified home inspector reviews every one, and you get a printable report ready to sign.

Maintenance

Tenants send a request from their phone any time. You get an email right away, Tello suggests the priority and next steps, and both of you can see the status until it's fixed.

Tenant portal

Invite your tenant by email. They get their own secure portal with their home, lease dates, recorded rent payments, shared documents and a one-tap way to request repairs.

Leases + notices

Pick a California template and EC Rental fills in the property, tenant and rent details. Edit, print or save as PDF, and share it to your tenant's portal.

Rent + tax reports

Record rent, expenses and owner payments as they happen. EC Rental totals everything per property and exports a clean CSV for your accountant.
