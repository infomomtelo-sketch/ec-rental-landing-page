Questions

Good to know.

Who can list?

Owners and authorized managers only. Every listing must be for a home you have the right to rent out.

Is it on Zillow today?

The feed is built in Zillow's rental format. Listings appear on Zillow once EC Rental's feed partnership is approved.

Can I take a listing down?

Yes. Mark it rented or move it back to draft and it leaves the site and the feed.
