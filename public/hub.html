Questions

What is Tello?

Tello is an AI assistant for small businesses. It answers customers' questions on your website around the clock, takes the details of anyone who wants a quote or a callback, briefs you each day on who is waiting, and makes short videos.

How much does Tello cost?

Tello for your business is $19 a month for Starter and $49 a month for Pro. Both start with a 7-day free trial and you can cancel any time. The video maker is free.

How do I add Tello to my website?

Paste your website link on tello.live/business. Tello reads it and builds your assistant, and you add it to your site with one line of code or share your own Tello chat link.

Can Tello make mistakes?

Yes. Tello is an AI and can be wrong. It answers only from the information a business gives it, tells customers it is an AI, and offers to pass questions to the business.

Is the Tello video maker free?

Yes. Chat a few words and paste your web address, and Tello builds a short narrated video for free.

Can health care businesses use Tello?

No. Tello is not available for health care or medical businesses.
