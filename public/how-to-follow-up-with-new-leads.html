How to follow up with new leads quickly

A lead is a person who asked for something. A simple routine keeps them from slipping away.

Reply while they are still interested

People often contact several businesses and go with the one that answers helpfully first. You do not have to answer in seconds, but the sooner you reply, the better your chance.

A simple follow-up routine

  1. 1. Say thanksUse their name and mention what they asked about.
  2. 2. Answer the questionGive the answer or tell them when you will have it.
  3. 3. Ask one questionMake it easy to reply, for example the best time to talk.
  4. 4. Set the next stepOffer a quote, a visit or a call, with a date.

Keep track of who is waiting

Every lead needs an owner and a status. A spreadsheet works. What matters is that you can see, every day, who is still waiting for a reply.

If they do not reply

Follow up once or twice, a few days apart, then stop. Be polite and make it easy for them to say no.

Frequently asked questions

How fast should I reply to a new lead?

As soon as you reasonably can. The faster you reply, the better your chance of keeping their interest. Set a goal you can keep, such as the same business day.

How many times should I follow up?

Once or twice, a few days apart. Then stop, and leave the door open.

What should I say in a first reply?

Thank them, answer what they asked, ask one simple question and suggest the next step.

How do I stop leads slipping away?

Write down every lead, give each a status and check the list every day.
