How it works

      

        
Paste your link
Tello reads your website and writes notes about your services, prices, hours and contact details.

        
Add one line
A chat button appears on your site. Customers ask questions and get answers from your own words.

        
Get the leads
When someone asks for a quote or a callback, Tello takes their details and emails them to you.

      

      
Tello only answers from what is on your site and the notes you add, and says so when it is not sure. You can read every lead and edit what Tello knows any time.
