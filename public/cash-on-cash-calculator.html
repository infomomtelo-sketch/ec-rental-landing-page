What the numbers mean

Cash-on-cash return
Yearly cash flow divided by the cash you put in. It's the return on your own money, after the loan payment.
Cash flow
Rent minus every monthly cost, including the mortgage. Positive means the rental pays you each month.
Why it differs from cap rate
Cap rate ignores the loan. Cash-on-cash includes it, so a bigger loan can raise or lower it depending on the interest rate.

These are estimates from the numbers you enter, not financial, tax or lending advice. Want every number in one place? Try the rental property calculator or the cap rate calculator.
