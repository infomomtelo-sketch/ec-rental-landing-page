California rent increase calculator

California's Tenant Protection Act (AB 1482) caps rent increases on many rentals at 5% plus the regional CPI change, and never more than 10% in any 12 months. Enter the rent to see your limit and the notice you need. It's free and there's no sign-up.

What the numbers mean

The cap
Over any 12 months, rent can rise by 5% plus the regional CPI change, or 10%, whichever is lower. It's measured from the lowest rent in the past 12 months, with at most two increases in that time.
Notice
Give 30 days' written notice for a raise of 10% or less, and 90 days for more than 10% (only possible on exempt properties).
Local rent control
Some cities, like Los Angeles, San Francisco and Oakland, have stricter limits. Where a local rule is stricter, it applies instead.
